CALCULATIONS & FORMULAS INCLUDED
================================================================================

Channel Attention:
   z_c = (1/HW) Σ F(i,j,c)
   M_c(F) = σ(W_2 δ(W_1 z))
   F_c = F ⊙ M_c(F)

Spatial Attention:
   M_s(F) = σ(Conv_7×7([Mean_c(F) ∥ Max_c(F)]))
   F'_s = F ⊙ M_s(F)

Self-Attention:
   A = softmax(QK^T / √d)
   Y = AV
   F_SA = F + γ reshape(Y)

Loss Function:
   L_CE = -(1/N) Σ_i Σ_c y_i,c log(ŷ_i,c)

Evaluation Metrics:
   Precision_c = TP_c / (TP_c + FP_c)
   Recall_c = TP_c / (TP_c + FN_c)
   F1_c = 2(P_c × R_c)/(P_c + R_c)
   Macro F1 = (1/C) Σ F1_c

Grad-CAM:
   α_c_k = (1/HW) Σ ∂y_c/∂A^k_ij
   L_Grad-CAM = ReLU(Σ α_c_k A^k)
